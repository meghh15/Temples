About Amruteshwara Temple

📍 Location

Amruteshwara Temple is located in Amruthapura, Chikkamagaluru District, Karnataka.

🛕 Description

Amruteshwara Temple is a historic temple known for its beautiful architecture and artistic stone carvings. It is an important example of the architectural heritage of Karnataka.

✨ Speciality

The temple is famous for its detailed sculptures, decorative pillars, beautiful stone work and traditional architectural design.

🙏 Main Deity

Lord Amruteshwara, a form of Lord Shiva, is the main deity worshipped in the temple.

🌿 Place

Amruthapura, Chikkamagaluru District, Karnataka.
