About Nanjangud Temple

📍 Location

Nanjangud, Mysuru District, Karnataka

🛕 Description

Srikanteshwara Temple is a famous historic temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is situated on the banks of the Kapila River and is an important pilgrimage centre of Karnataka.

✨ Speciality

The temple is known for its impressive architecture, large gopurams and rich religious traditions. Nanjangud is also popularly known as the Kashi of the South.

🙏 Main Deity

Lord Srikanteshwara (Lord Shiva)

🌿 Place

Nanjangud, Mysuru District, Karnataka
