About Aihole Durga Temple

📍 Location

Aihole, Bagalkot, Karnataka

🛕 Description

Durga Temple is one of the famous ancient monuments of Aihole. Aihole is known as an important centre of early Chalukyan architecture and is home to many historic temples.

✨ Speciality

The temple is famous for its unique apsidal shape, stone pillars and beautiful architectural design. It is an important example of the architectural experiments of the Chalukyan period.

🙏 Main Deity

Goddess Durga

🌿 Place

Aihole, Bagalkot District, Karnataka
