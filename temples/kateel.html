✦ Kateel Durga Parameshwari Temple ✦

A Sacred Temple on the Nandini River

About the Temple

📍 Location

Kateel, Dakshina Kannada, Karnataka

🛕 Description

Kateel Durga Parameshwari Temple is a famous pilgrimage centre in Karnataka. The temple is dedicated to Goddess Durga Parameshwari and is situated on an island in the Nandini River.

✨ Speciality

The temple is known for its spiritual importance, beautiful natural surroundings and traditional cultural practices. Yakshagana performances are also an important part of the temple tradition.

🙏 Main Deity

Goddess Durga Parameshwari

🌿 Place

Kateel, Dakshina Kannada District, Karnataka
