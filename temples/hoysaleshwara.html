About Hoysaleshwara Temple

📍 Location

Hoysaleshwara Temple is located in Halebidu, Hassan District, Karnataka.

🛕 Description

Hoysaleshwara Temple is a famous historic temple and one of the finest examples of Hoysala architecture in Karnataka. The temple is known for its artistic beauty and detailed stone carvings.

✨ Speciality

The temple is famous for its highly detailed sculptures, beautiful friezes, decorative pillars and intricate carvings depicting stories from Hindu mythology and everyday life.

🙏 Main Deity

Lord Hoysaleshwara, a form of Lord Shiva, is the main deity of the temple.

🌿 Place

Halebidu, Hassan District, Karnataka.
