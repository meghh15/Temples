About Karnataka Temples

Where Heritage Meets Devotion

Karnataka is a land of rich history, beautiful architecture, ancient traditions and spiritual heritage. Its temples are not only places of worship but also magnificent examples of art, culture and history.

Where Heritage Meets Devotion Karnataka is a land where history, art, architecture, and spirituality come together. Its ancient temples stand as timeless symbols of faith and beautifully preserve the stories and traditions of generations. Temples of Karnataka is a small digital journey through these sacred places. Our aim is to showcase the beauty, history, architecture, festivals, and cultural significance of Karnataka’s temples in one place. From magnificent stone carvings to peaceful sanctuaries, every temple has a story waiting to be discovered. Explore the Heritage • Feel the Tradition • Discover the Divine

This website is created to explore and showcase the famous temples of Karnataka. From the magnificent Virupaksha Temple to the peaceful temples of Udupi, Sringeri, Dharmasthala and Kollur, every temple has its own unique story and tradition.

Our aim is to help visitors discover the beauty, history, architecture, festivals and cultural importance of Karnataka's temples in one place.
